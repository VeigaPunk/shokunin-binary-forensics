2. **A polyglot negative-control case.** None of the five capabilities is tested against a file that is valid as two formats at once (for example a PDF that is also a ZIP, or a PNG with an appended archive). A short note listing where each in-scope format declares its byte order and integer encoding would give the container cases a shared pre-flight check.
