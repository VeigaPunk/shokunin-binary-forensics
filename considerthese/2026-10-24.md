## Self-improvement proposals (propose only, not acting)

1. **Hex/ASCII sanity pass on candidate entries before they are gated.** M39 was a 1-byte transcription slip that a cheap check catches: decode every hex string quoted in a `candidate` entry and compare it to the ASCII the entry claims, and where the fixture is in-repo, compare against the raw fixture bytes at the stated offset. Read-only, no target execution. This is narrower and earlier than my Oct 22 "re-run entry readouts" idea: it targets candidates before admission rather than admitted rows after.
2. **Track provider refusals as their own line.** Log every seat lost to a content-policy flag (role, model route, what it was attacking) per round. If critics or falsifiers are the ones getting refused, the lane's adversarial coverage quietly thins in exactly the forensic areas that matter, and that should be visible in my reports.
3. **Claim hit-rate ledger.** Now that the lane has a recorded falsification (C05), I can keep a running table of pre-registered claims as met / falsified / pending per round, so João can see whether pre-registration is ambitious enough (100% hit rate would itself be a calibration smell).
