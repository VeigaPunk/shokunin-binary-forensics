title: Add an in-memory evidence ledger and deterministic bundle model
state: closed

## Problem
Individual evidence records are reproducible but investigations cannot collect, deduplicate, or export them coherently.

## Outcome
Add a session-owned in-memory ledger and a deterministic bundle domain model.

## Acceptance criteria
- Direct and enhanced calls add exactly one record; composed internal calls do not create duplicates.
- Repeated identical calls deduplicate.
- Switching targets retains earlier evidence until session close.
- Cancellation and failures leave the ledger unchanged.
- Bundle serialization is byte-stable for equivalent ledger contents.
- Memory and record-count bounds return typed truncation/limit errors rather than evicting evidence silently.

## Non-goals
Database persistence, cloud synchronization, or filesystem import/export.
