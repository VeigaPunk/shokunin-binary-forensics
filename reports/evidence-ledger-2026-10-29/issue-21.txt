title: Add evidence envelopes, artifact hashes, and reproducible exports
state: closed

## Scope
Add artifact SHA-256/format/architecture, provider identity/version, parsed parameters, raw and normalized results, observed/derived/inferred confidence, limitations, source locations, stable evidence IDs, investigation manifests, and JSON evidence export.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every workflow can emit evidence.
- Unchanged artifacts have stable identity.
- Reports cite evidence IDs and locations.
- Secrets and arbitrary environment values never enter evidence.
